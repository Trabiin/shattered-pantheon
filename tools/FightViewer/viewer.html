Turns: faster fighters act more often. Each turn a fighter uses its ultimate if its mana bar (thin, under health) is full, otherwise its first ready skill, otherwise its basic attack.

Cover: a front fighter shields the back fighters standing behind it. When it falls, those back fighters are exposed and most attacks can reach them.

Health bar: green is health, pale blue is shield. Shields absorb damage first.

Effects: blue chips are buffs, orange chips are debuffs. The number is turns left.
